My 90,000-parameter model writes algebra that looks right 94% of the time. It is right 8% of the time.

It has been a strange week. Researchers showed a frontier model can read a small neural network's weights and write new ones by hand, no training. Within a day people were trying it on their own problems, including a hand-written network that tells real legal citations from fake ones.

Interesting result. Good moment for a plainer question: what can a checker like that actually see?

PERFECT LAYOUT, WRONG ANSWER

My demo model was trained on correctly worked equations, all laid out the same way. Ask it to solve 7x + 2 = 16 and it writes:

7x = 18 => x = 3

The layout is perfect. The working is wrong from the first step: 16 minus 2 is 14.

Across all 800 equations of the kind it was trained on, 94% of its answers look like valid working and 8.4% are right. Most of them were in its training text word for word.

THE DOUBT NEVER REACHES THE PAGE

At the moment it writes the wrong digit, the model gives 8 a 31% chance. The right answer, 4, is second at 18%. Inside, it is close to a coin toss. On the page there is only a clean, confident 18.

WHAT A CHECKER OF FORM CAN CATCH

A checker that looks only at the line (right shape, sensible numbers) would pass nearly all of these wrong answers. To catch 7x = 18 you have to do the arithmetic.

Citations are the same. Fakes that give themselves away in the form, or do not exist at all, are the easy ones. The ones that reach a judge look exactly right: a real case at the wrong page, or a real case that does not say what it is cited for. A Bloomberg study this year found models caught 93-100% of citations to the wrong case, but only 37-61% of citations to the wrong page of the right case. They missed them because the case was on topic.

So when a small network tells real citations from fake ones, the useful question is not whether its weights were trained or hand-written. It is what the fakes looked like. A checker that only sees the citation can only learn what fake citations look like.

CHECK AGAINST THE CONTENT

For algebra, the fix is a calculator. For law, it is the law itself, organised to be checked: each point of law linked to the case, page and passage that holds it, and to the later cases that followed or overruled it. A knowledge graph of points of law. Then checking a citation is a lookup, not a judgement about how it looks.

And a check grounded in the source does not move every time the model does.

If you are buying or building citation checking, ask one question: what does the check consult? If the answer is the citation alone, it is checking form.
